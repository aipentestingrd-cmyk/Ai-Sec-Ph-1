THE HACKFEST

What a weekend at AI-SEC HackFest looks like

Hands-on, not hypothetical

You attack a real, deliberately vulnerable AI system — an LLM-backed app with a RAG pipeline and agentic tool calls, built to be broken.

Find it, then fix it

Document the exploit your team finds, then flip to defense and ship a working fix — a filter, a monitor, a hardened design.

Mentors sit with you, not above you

Practitioners in red teaming, LLM security, and MCP rotate through office hours across both days of the build.

You leave with a repo, not a slide deck

Every team demos working code live — judges score depth and real-world impact over polish.
