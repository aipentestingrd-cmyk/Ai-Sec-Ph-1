Why we exist

Vision & Mission

Vision

To build a globally recognized community that empowers individuals and organizations to securely adopt, develop, and defend AI technologies, fostering a safer digital future through collaboration, innovation, and continuous learning.

Mission

To create an inclusive platform for learning, collaboration, and knowledge sharing in AI Security and AI for Security — hands-on workshops, CTF challenges, meetups, technical sessions, boardroom discussions, and flash mentorship — bridging theory and real-world practice.
